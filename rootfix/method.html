The 15 checks

What we look at.

Five places a customer meets you before they speak to you. Three checks in each.

01

Website

  1. Loads in under 3 seconds on a phone
  2. Phone number and WhatsApp can be tapped
  3. Each page has one clear next step

02

Google listing

  1. Claimed, in the right category
  2. Hours, photos and services filled in
  3. Shows up for searches near you

03

WhatsApp

  1. Business profile and catalogue set up
  2. How long a reply takes
  3. Saved answers for common questions

04

Reviews

  1. How many, and the rating
  2. Whether the owner replies
  3. Whether anyone is asked for one

05

Ads

  1. Where the click lands
  2. Price and next step on that page
  3. Whether leads are counted
