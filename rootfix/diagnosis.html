FAQ

Plain answers.

How much does it cost?

The diagnosis is free. A one-page site is ₹4,999. A shop website starts at ₹8,000 and a WhatsApp bot at ₹10,000. You get a fixed price in writing before any work starts. GST is extra where it applies.

How long does a fix take?

Small fixes such as a tappable phone number or a Google profile take a day. A shop website takes 3 to 5 days. A larger site with 5 to 8 pages takes 7 to 14 days.

Do you work outside India?

Yes. Work is done online and priced in US dollars for clients abroad. A business website starts at $250.

What if I already have a website?

Then the diagnosis starts there. Often the site is fine and the root is a missing button, a slow page or an empty Google listing. We fix that and leave the rest alone.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You send your logo, photos and business details on WhatsApp. We build it, show you, and hand over something you can update yourself.

What happens after the diagnosis?

You get a short report: the symptoms we found, the one root behind them, and the fixes in order with prices. You can do them with us, with someone else, or not at all.
