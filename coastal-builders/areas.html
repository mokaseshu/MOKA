Service areas

Building across Visakhapatnam, from Bheemili to Anakapalli.

Local knowledge matters: soil, slopes, salt air, approvals and even which roads a concrete truck can use. Here is what we know about each area.

Schematic · Bay of Bengal coastline

01

Builders in MVP Colony

Our head office is here, so most MVP Colony sites are a 10-minute drive for our engineers. We renovate many of the colony's older independent houses and build G+2 homes on the compact sector plots, keeping neighbours happy with dust screens and tidy sites.

Free site visit in MVP Colony
02

Builders in Madhurawada

Vizag's fastest-growing suburb, with new layouts and wide plots near the IT corridor. We build a lot of G+1 family homes here and know the soil conditions across the hill slopes and the low-lying stretches near the highway.

Free site visit in Madhurawada
03

Builders in Rushikonda

Sea views, slopes and salty air. Rushikonda homes need stepped foundations, corrosion-resistant concrete and smart shading. Our villa projects here use coastal-grade specifications as standard.

Free site visit in Rushikonda
04

Builders in Gajuwaka

A busy, established neighbourhood where many homes are 20+ years old. We do a lot of terrace waterproofing, extra-floor additions and full renovations for Gajuwaka families.

Free site visit in Gajuwaka
05

Builders in Seethammadhara

Central, premium and mostly built-up, so our work here is mainly interiors, modular kitchens and careful renovations in occupied homes and apartments.

Free site visit in Seethammadhara
06

Builders in PM Palem

Growing quickly along the main road, with a mix of commercial frontage and new houses behind. We have built retail and office blocks and independent homes across PM Palem.

Free site visit in PM Palem
07

Builders in Bheemili

Beach-side plots and weekend homes. Bheemili needs careful planning around coastal regulation rules, high winds and humidity, which we factor into design and material choices.

Free site visit in Bheemili
08

Builders in Anakapalli

Larger plots and family homes at a better price per sq yard. We run full turnkey projects here with the same site engineer and weekly update system we use in the city.

Free site visit in Anakapalli
