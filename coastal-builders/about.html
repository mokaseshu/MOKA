About us · Since 2012

A Vizag builder that writes everything down.

Fourteen years, 180+ projects and one simple rule: no surprises for the family paying for the home.

Our story

Started by a site engineer who was tired of excuses.

Ravi Varma spent a decade as a site engineer on Vizag projects before starting Coastal Builders in 2012. He saw the same problems again and again: vague estimates, missing materials, months of delay and families who could not get a straight answer.

Coastal Builders was built around fixing that. Fixed-price agreements. Named brands. A dedicated engineer on every site. Photos every Saturday. Today we are a team of 40+ engineers, supervisors and craftspeople, and most of our new work comes from families who were referred by someone we built for.

The team

40+ people. One phone number.

Civil engineers, site supervisors, carpenters, masons, electricians and plumbers, most of whom have worked with us for over five years. Every project has one engineer who owns it end to end.

Values

Four rules we don’t break.

  1. 01

    Write it down

    Prices, brands, schedules and changes. If it matters, it is in writing.

  2. 02

    Show the work

    Weekly photos and open site visits. Nothing is hidden behind plaster.

  3. 03

    Build for the coast

    Vizag's salt air and monsoons need better concrete, cover and waterproofing. We build for it as standard.

  4. 04

    Leave it clean

    Tidy sites, safe sites and good relations with your neighbours.
