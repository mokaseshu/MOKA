Ask on WhatsApp
What is the construction cost per sq ft in Vizag?

Our house construction packages start from ₹1,850 per sq ft of built-up area. Most families finish between ₹2,000 and ₹2,600 per sq ft depending on finishes. The final price is fixed in writing after a free site visit.

How long does it take to build a house?

A 1,500–2,500 sq ft G+1 house usually takes 9 to 11 months. We give you a week-by-week schedule before work starts, and our average project finishes 11 days ahead of it.

How do the payment stages work?

You pay in stages linked to completed work, not dates: agreement, foundation, each slab, brickwork, plastering, flooring and handover. You never pay for work that is not yet done.

Do you help with GVMC / VMRDA plan approval?

Yes. We prepare the building plan drawings and assist with the GVMC or VMRDA approval process, including the documents you need to submit.

Which materials do you use?

Branded cement (UltraTech, Ramco or similar), Fe-550D TMT steel, ISI-marked plumbing and electrical items and branded paints. Brands are listed in your agreement.

What warranty do I get?

A 10-year structural warranty, 7 years on waterproofing and 1 year on fittings and finishes, all in writing (sample terms for this concept).
