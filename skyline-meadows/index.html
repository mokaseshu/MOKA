08FAQ

What buyers ask before they visit.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is Skyline Meadows approved?

Yes. The layout is approved by APCRDA, the Amaravati Capital Region Development Authority, and registered with AP RERA under P06XXXXXXXXXXX. We share copies of both before you book.

Can I get a bank loan for a plot here?

Yes. APCRDA-approved plots are eligible for plot loans from major nationalised and private banks, usually up to 75–80% of the plot value. Our team helps you with the paperwork.

How does registration work?

Once the balance is paid, the sale deed is registered in your name at the local Sub-Registrar office, usually the same week. Stamp duty and registration charges are extra, at Andhra Pradesh government rates.

What is the payment plan?

Book with ₹1 lakh. Pay 30% of the plot value within 30 days, and the balance within 90 days at registration. If you take a bank loan, the bank pays the balance directly.

When will development be complete?

Phase 1 roads, drainage, water lines, street lights and the compound wall are complete. The central park, walking track and avenue plantation are being finished now. Phase 2 opens after Phase 1 sells out.

How do site visits and pickup work?

Visits run on weekends: Sat 10 AM, Sat 4 PM, Sun 10 AM and Sun 4 PM. We pick you up from Vijayawada and drop you back at no cost. If you live outside India, choose a live video tour instead.
