How to buy

From site visit to registration, in five steps.

  1. 01

    Visit the site

    Walk the layout with our team and see the plots, roads and amenities on the ground.

  2. 02

    Choose your plot

    Compare size, facing and road width, and hold the plot number you want.

  3. 03

    Check documents

    Review the link documents, layout approval and encumbrance certificate with your lawyer.

  4. 04

    Book

    Pay the booking amount and receive a written allotment for your plot number.

  5. 05

    Register

    Complete payment and register the sale deed at the sub-registrar office.
