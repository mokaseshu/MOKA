Ask on WhatsApp
Will my treatment be painful?

For almost everyone, no. We apply a numbing gel before any injection, use slow computer-paced anaesthesia, and check in with you throughout. If you're very anxious, we also offer gentle conscious sedation.

How much will my treatment cost?

A consultation is ₹300 and includes a digital X-ray if needed. After your exam you'll get a written plan with the exact price for each step — no hidden charges. Indicative prices are listed on each treatment page.

Do you offer EMI?

Yes. Treatments above ₹10,000 can be split into 3, 6 or 9 months at 0% interest through select credit cards and finance partners. We also accept UPI, cards, net banking and cash.

How long does the first visit take?

About 30–40 minutes. That covers a full check-up, X-rays if needed, and a calm conversation about your options. If you need urgent pain relief, we treat it the same day.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. For severe pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a knocked-out tooth, call us right away — we keep emergency slots open every day during clinic hours.

Do you treat children?

We love little patients. We recommend a first visit by age one or when the first tooth appears. Our kids' visits are short, playful and focused on making them feel safe.
