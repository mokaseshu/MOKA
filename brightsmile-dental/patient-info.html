What to bring

Three things that help.

ID & contact details

Any photo ID and a phone number for WhatsApp reminders. For children, a parent or guardian should accompany them.

Medical history

  • Current medicines (photo of strips is fine)
  • Allergies, especially to anaesthetics
  • Conditions like diabetes, BP or heart issues
  • Pregnancy, if applicable

Previous dental records

Old X-rays, reports or treatment notes help us avoid repeating tests. Please bring them in person — don't send them over WhatsApp.

Dental emergency

Severe pain or a broken tooth? Call now.

We keep emergency slots open every day during clinic hours. Until you reach us:

  1. Knocked-out tooth: hold it by the crown, rinse gently, keep it in milk and come in within 1 hour.
  2. Swelling: use a cold compress outside the cheek — never heat.
  3. Severe toothache: rinse with warm salt water and take a regular painkiller you know is safe for you.
  4. Bleeding or facial swelling with fever: go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Cancellation policy

Plans change. That's okay.

Because we keep appointments on time for everyone, we just ask for a little notice.

  • Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 4 hours before your appointment — a WhatsApp message is enough.
  • If you're running late, let us know. We'll hold your slot for 15 minutes, or move you to the next available time.
  • For long treatments (implants, aligner scans), we may ask for a small refundable booking deposit.
  • Unwell, or have a fever? Please reschedule — it protects our team and other patients.
