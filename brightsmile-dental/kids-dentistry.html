Questions

Kids Dentistry FAQs

When should my child first see a dentist?

By their first birthday or when the first tooth appears. Early visits are mostly about getting comfortable and catching problems early.

My child is very scared. What will you do?

We take it slowly, explain with toys and mirrors, and stop whenever your child needs a break. Many nervous children need one ‘just looking’ visit first — and that's perfectly fine.

Do milk teeth really need fillings?

Yes. Milk teeth hold space for adult teeth and help with chewing and speech. Untreated decay can cause pain and infection.

How much is a kids' dental check-up in Guntur?

A children's check-up at BrightSmile Dental starts from ₹500. Fluoride and sealants are priced separately and explained before we start.

Can I stay with my child during treatment?

Always. Parents are welcome to sit beside their child for every visit.
