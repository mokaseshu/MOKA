Questions

Teeth Whitening FAQs

Is teeth whitening safe for enamel?

Yes, when done professionally. We use regulated gels, protect your gums and limit exposure time, so your enamel stays healthy.

Will whitening make my teeth sensitive?

Some people feel mild sensitivity for 24–48 hours. We use desensitising agents before and after to keep it minimal.

How long do whitening results last?

Typically 1–2 years, depending on your diet and habits. Occasional touch-ups with your take-home kit keep teeth bright.

What does teeth whitening cost in Guntur?

In-clinic whitening at BrightSmile Dental starts from ₹6,000, including a take-home touch-up kit.

Will whitening work on crowns or fillings?

Whitening only lightens natural teeth. If you have visible crowns or fillings, we'll plan around them so your smile looks even.
