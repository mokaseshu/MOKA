What happens, exactly.

No surprises. Here's how your Root Canal treatment usually goes.

Questions

Root Canal FAQs

Does a root canal hurt?

With modern anaesthesia, a root canal feels similar to getting a filling. The infection is what causes pain — the treatment relieves it.

Can a root canal be done in one sitting?

In most cases, yes. Severe infections occasionally need two visits so medication can settle the tooth first.

Do I need a crown after a root canal?

For back teeth, usually yes. A treated tooth becomes more brittle, and a crown protects it from cracking.

What is the root canal cost in Guntur?

Root canal treatment starts from ₹3,500 per tooth at BrightSmile Dental. Molars and re-treatments cost more, and crowns are priced separately.

What if I just remove the tooth instead?

Extraction is possible, but a missing tooth can cause shifting and bite problems, and replacing it later usually costs more than saving it.
