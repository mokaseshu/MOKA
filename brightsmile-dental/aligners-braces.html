Questions

Aligners & Braces FAQs

Aligners or braces — which is better for me?

Both work well. Aligners are nearly invisible and removable, which suits most adults. Braces can be better for complex bites or for teens who may forget to wear aligners. We'll recommend honestly after your assessment.

Do aligners hurt?

You may feel gentle pressure for a day or two when you switch to a new set — that's the aligners working. It's far more comfortable than traditional braces.

How many hours a day do I need to wear aligners?

About 20–22 hours a day. You take them out to eat, drink anything other than water, and brush.

What is the cost of aligners in Guntur?

Braces start from ₹35,000 and clear aligners from ₹55,000 for full treatment. Mild cases often cost less. You'll receive a written quote after your 3D scan.

Am I too old for teeth straightening?

Not at all. Many of our aligner patients are in their 30s, 40s and 50s. As long as your gums are healthy, teeth can be moved at any age.
