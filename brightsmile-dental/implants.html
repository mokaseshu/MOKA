Step by step

What happens, exactly.

No surprises. Here's how your Dental Implants treatment usually goes.

  1. Day 1 · 40 min

    Consultation & 3D scan

    We check your bone and gums with a low-dose 3D scan and plan the exact position of your implant.

  2. Day 7 · 60 min

    Gentle implant placement

    Under local anaesthesia, a small titanium post is placed. Most patients say it felt easier than an extraction.

  3. 8–12 weeks

    Healing period

    The implant fuses with your bone. You wear a temporary tooth, so you're never without a smile.

  4. Final visit · 30 min

    Final crown fitted

    A custom-shaded ceramic crown is fixed on top. It blends in perfectly with your natural teeth.

Questions

Dental Implants FAQs

Is getting a dental implant painful?

Placement is done under local anaesthesia, so you feel pressure but not pain. Afterwards most patients need only a mild painkiller for a day or two.

How long does the whole implant process take?

Usually 2–4 months from start to finish, because the implant needs time to bond with your bone. You'll have a temporary tooth during this time.

Am I too old for an implant?

There's no upper age limit. If your general health is stable and you have enough bone (which we check on the 3D scan), implants work well at any adult age.

How much does a dental implant cost in Guntur?

At BrightSmile Dental, a single implant with crown starts from ₹25,000. Your exact price depends on the implant system and whether bone grafting is needed — we confirm it in writing before we begin.

Can I pay for implants in EMI?

Yes. Implants can be split into 3, 6 or 9 monthly payments at 0% interest through select cards and finance partners.
