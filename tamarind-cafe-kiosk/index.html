Problem

Between 8:30–10 AM and 5–7 PM, one cashier takes every order, and most of the wait is people deciding, customising and paying.

In a busy café, the queue is slow because ordering and paying happen at the same spot, not because the coffee is slow. Customers read a wall menu from far away, ask about sizes and milk, then fumble for UPI. Some walk out. The cashier also can't upsell when there's a line.

Every number on this page is a research assumption or a design target. This is a concept, so nothing here comes from a live product.

Research

Survey questions (Google Form)

  1. What time do you usually visit a café, and how long do you wait to order?
  2. Have you ever left a café queue without ordering? Why?
  3. What slows you down at the counter: menu, choices, or payment?
  4. Do you change size, milk or sugar in your drink?
  5. How do you prefer to pay: UPI, card or cash?
  6. Would you use a self-order screen if it were faster? What would stop you?

Observe for 30 minutes at peak

  • Time from joining the queue to paying
  • How many people ask a question before ordering
  • How many leave the queue
  • Most-ordered items and common changes
  • Time spent on payment alone

Expected findings

Most of the time goes on decidingCustomers spend longer reading the wall menu and asking questions than the cashier spends punching in the order.
Customising is the slowest question"Is there oat milk?", "Less sugar?" and "Which size?" are asked again and again, and the answers are always the same.
UPI is the default, but it failsScanning a small counter QR, typing the amount and waiting for confirmation causes the most awkward pauses.

Insight: show every choice as a big tap target with the price beside it. Then customers answer their own questions, and the cashier only hands over the order.

User flow

  1. Eat here or take awayOne tap starts the order
  2. Pick itemsPhoto grid, cart always visible
  3. CustomiseSize, milk, sugar as big buttons
  4. ReviewOne relevant add-on, change quantities
  5. PayQR with exact amount, card or counter
  6. Get tokenBig number, ready time, auto reset
Edge paths: UPI fails or times out, so the screen offers retry, card or pay at counter without losing the order. No touch for 60 seconds shows "Still ordering?", and after 15 more seconds it clears the cart for the next person.

Kiosk rules

  • Tap targets at least 52px, main buttons 62–132px
  • Main actions in the lower two-thirds of the screen
  • No typing anywhere in the order flow
  • Lucide icons, 2px stroke, always with a text label
  • Idle reset after 60 s, with a warning first
