About Sri Sai

A tuition centre run by teachers

Started in 2012 in Brodipet, Guntur, with eleven students. Today five full-time teachers teach Class 6 to Intermediate.

Founder

Mr. S. Venkata Rao

Maths · 22 years teaching · M.Sc., B.Ed.

A parent should never have to guess how their child is doing.

Before Sri Sai, he taught Maths at two junior colleges in Guntur. He started the centre to keep batches small enough to correct every notebook.

Facilities

AC classrooms

Six air-conditioned rooms with smart boards and good lighting.

Science lab

Physics, Chemistry and Biology practicals for Class 9 to Inter.

Reference books, previous papers and a silent study hall.

Weekly tests

Board-pattern tests every Sunday with answer-sheet review.

Group study

Revision tables where seniors help juniors before exams.
